A proposed solar water intervention for Laikipia's Loisiwa area, developed with the County Government, LCDA, Zeitz Foundation and Medical 3T. An 11-member water committee is formed and governance training complete; three candidate sites remain under review.

The challenge

Lack of access to safe and reliable water for the Loisiwa community.
