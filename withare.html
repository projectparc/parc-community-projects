2. Purpose and objective

The Withare community, which practises crop and livestock farming, needed reliable water for households and livestock. PARC solarised the community borehole to supply a water collection point and livestock trough, offsetting an estimated 9,100 L of diesel a year.

3. Scope

  • Included: Supply and installation of a 9.8 kW solar PV array (18 x 545W Jinko panels) and Lorentz PSK3-15 (11 kVA) controller driving a 5 kW pump sized for 25 m³/day; water collection point and livestock trough; site fencing; testing and commissioning (contractor: Centre for Alternative Technologies).
  • Excluded: Routine day-to-day operation, which is the Water Committee's responsibility; scheduled maintenance is provided separately under the O&M arrangement with CAT.

6. Community and partner commitments

The Withare Water Committee provided site access and input on siting, and manages the water point. Ol Pejeta Conservancy partnered with PARC on the project. Centre for Alternative Technologies delivered the works and provides scheduled maintenance.

7. Operations and sustainability

The Withare Water Committee operates the system, with biannual scheduled maintenance by CAT and support from PARC's field team.

8. Main risks

  • Damage to taps, trough chamber and fencing — managed through regular site checks and repairs by the contractor and committee.
  • Livestock pressure at the trough — managed by the Water Committee through watering schedules.

9. Measures of success

  • System commissioned and handed over to the community
  • Reliable daily supply within the design capacity (25 m³/day)
  • Scheduled maintenance carried out and logged
  • Community and livestock water access maintained year-round
